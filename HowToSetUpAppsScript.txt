1. Make sure your spreadsheet is a google sheet and not an excel spreadsheet
2. Click on "Extensions"
3. Click on "Apps Script"
4. Write your code
5. Click "Deploy" in the top right
6. Deploy as a web app
7. Set execute as to "me(email.email@gmail.com)"
8. Set who has access to "anyone"
9. Go to "Manage Deployments" under "Deploy" and copy the web app url
10. Use this in your program
